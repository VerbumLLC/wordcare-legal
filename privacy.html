Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Word Care ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Verbum LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Word Care mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using Word Care, you agree to this policy.

Age requirement: Word Care is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information that you provide directly:

  • Account information: your name and email address when you create an account.
  • Your content: journal entries, prayer reflections, mood check-ins, and other personal reflections you choose to write in the app.
  • Payment information: if you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store your full payment card details on our servers.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information such as device type, app version, and basic usage data needed to operate and improve the Service.

Sensitive Information

Some content you create (such as prayer reflections or journal entries) may reveal religious beliefs or personal feelings. We treat this information as sensitive and protect it accordingly. You choose what to write, and you can delete your entries or your entire account at any time.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Service, including saving and syncing your journal entries across your devices.
  • To generate AI-assisted reflections and prayers based on content you submit.
  • To manage your account, authentication, and subscription.
  • To communicate with you about your account (for example, email confirmation and password reset).
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the app, including:

  • Supabase – database, authentication, and storage of your account and journal data.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) – to generate AI-assisted reflections and prayers from content you submit. Content sent to Anthropic is processed to return a response and is handled in accordance with Anthropic's commercial data terms.
  • Resend – to send account-related emails.
  • Google Play Billing – to process subscription payments made through the Google Play Store. Google handles your payment information under its own privacy policy.

We do not currently use third-party analytics services. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy to describe them.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or Verbum LLC.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your journal entries, profile, and account data. Some information may persist briefly in backups before being removed.

Your Rights and Choices

  • You can view and edit your journal entries in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account and associated data directly within the app (Profile → Delete Account), or by contacting us at the email below.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these, contact us.

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including row-level security and encrypted connections, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Word Care is not directed to and may not be used by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
